When dealing with recurrences, we often omit the cases from the odd numbers and even numbers, that's because the result is the same. Also, the true recurrences states that we need floor and ceilings, however, if the result is the same we can ignore that.


<span style="color:yellow">Evidencia:</span>

When we see the recurrence, it's without floor and ceiling, but when the number it's odd, we don't have the middle. That's when the floor and ceiling appears, but, it's complicated dealing with that, so we ignore them, since the result does not change.
