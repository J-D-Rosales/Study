>[!tip] Theorem 7.31
>If $A \leq_{p} B \text{ and } B \in P,\text{ then} A \in P$

It just says that if A can be reduced to B and B is solved in polynomial time, therefore A can be solved in polynomial time, as far as the computable function is polynomial [[D-Polynomial time computable function]].
