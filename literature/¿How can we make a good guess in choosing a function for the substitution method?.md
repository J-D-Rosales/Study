<span style="color:yellow">IDEA:</span>
There isn't a formula for this. It mostly depends on the experience you have solving other recurrences, and the similarity with other problems you have done. So, to make a good guess you should take similar solutions to have more certainty.
Another way is making by the lower upper bound, $T(n) = \Omega(n)$, proving this, will take you certainty, and you will be reducing the space between the upper bound and the lower, until you finally get $T(n) = \Theta(nf(n))$.
