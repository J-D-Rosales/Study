<span style="color:yellow">IDEA:</span>
There are two types of heaps:
**Max-Heap**: It's characterized for have at the root the greater value, and the next property holds always $A[Parent(i)] \geq A[i]$.
**Min-Heap**: It's characterized for have at the root the minimum value, and the next property holds always $A[Parent(i)]\leq A[i]$. ^ef7cb6

<span style="color:yellow">Evidencia:</span>
We use Max-Heap for heapsort algorithm and min-heaps for priority queue constructions.
