<span style="color:yellow">IDEA:</span>
The divide and conquer approach follows the next. First to break the big problem into sub problems and be able to solve the sub problems recursively, so when you solve the sub problem you unite all the solution and have the solution for the bigger one.
The steps are basically:
**divide:** Divide the problem in small instances that are sub problems of the same problem. 
**conquer**: Solve the sub problems by solving them recursively. 
**combine**: Combine the solution of the sub problems to make the solution for the big problem. 

<span style="color:yellow">Evidencia:</span>
We can use that in the merge sort algorithm to have a better running time  in sorting arrays.
