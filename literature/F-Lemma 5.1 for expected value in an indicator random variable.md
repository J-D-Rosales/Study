>[!tip] Lemma 5.1
>Given a sample space S and a event A in the sample space S, let $X_{A} = I\{ A \}$. Then $E[X_{A}] = Pr\{ A \}$.

*Proof*:
We know:
$$
\begin{align}
E[X_{A}] = E[I\{ A \}] = 1\cdot Pr\{ A \}  + 0 Pr \{ \bar{A}\} = Pr\{ A \} 
\end{align}
$$
Where $\bar{A} = S- A$, the complement of A.
