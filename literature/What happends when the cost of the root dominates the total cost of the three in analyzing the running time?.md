<span style="color:yellow">IDEA:</span>
It means that the cost of the initial call of the algorithm is as much as cost as the rest of the recursion time. Therefore if the initial call is $O(f(n))$, and the total algorithm has $O(f(n))$ time as well, therefore the cost is $\Theta(f(n))$, because the first recursive makes a lower bound of $\Omega(n^{2})$.
