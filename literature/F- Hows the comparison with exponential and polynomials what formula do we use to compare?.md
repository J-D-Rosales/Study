From the definition of eulers number we can get the next propertie
$$
e^{x} \geq 1 + x
$$
When $|x| \leq 1$, we have the next approximation
$$
1+ x \leq e^{x} \leq 1 +x + x^{2}
$$
When $x\to 0$ the approximation is quite good:
$$
e^{x} = 1+x + \Theta(x^{2})
$$
