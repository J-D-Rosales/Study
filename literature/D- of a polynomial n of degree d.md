<span style="color:yellow">IDEA:</span>
A polynomial n of degree d is a function $p(n)$ of the form+
$$
p(i) = \sum _{i = 0}^{d} a_{i}n^{i}
$$
where the constants $a_{0},a_{1}\dots a_{d}$ are the coefficients of the polynomial and $a_{d} \not = 0$ .


<span style="color:yellow">Evidencia:</span>
If $a_{d} \geq 0$ The the polynomial is asymptotically positive and we have $p(n) = \Theta(n^{d})$.
For any real constant $a \geq 0$, the function $n^{a}$ is monotonically increasing and if $a \leq 0$ it's monotonically decreasing. 
A function is polynomially bounded if $f(n) = O(n^{k})$. for some constant k
