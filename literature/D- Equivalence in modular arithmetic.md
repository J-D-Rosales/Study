<span style="color:yellow">IDEA:</span>
If we have $a,b \in \mathbb{R}$ and $\text{a mod n} = \text{b mod n}$ means that the residue for both are the same, then we define equivalence:
$a  \equiv \text{b mod n}$ and $b \equiv a \text{ mod n}$
Also $a \equiv b \text{ mod n}$ if and only if $n$ is a divisor of $b-a$.
