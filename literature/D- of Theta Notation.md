<span style="color:yellow">IDEA:</span>
For a given function $g(n)$, we denote $\Theta(g(n))$ the *set of function* such that.
$$
\begin{align}
\Theta(g(n)) = \{ f(n) : \text{there exists two positive constant c1,c2 and n0 such that }  \\
0 \leq c_{1}g(n) \leq f(n) \leq c_{2} g(n) \text{ for all } n\geq n_{0}    \}
\end{align}

$$
