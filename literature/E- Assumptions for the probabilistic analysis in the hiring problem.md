In this problem, we use the next assumptions:
- Each applicant can come with the same probability to our interview.
- Each applicant come in random order.
In this problem,  random order means you can compare any of two candidates based on their *rank(i)*, where higher *rank(i)* corresponds to a better qualified applicant. Then, assuming that the ordered list *<rank(1),rank(2),...,rank(n)*,  that is it's 1 of the $<1,2,3,4,5,\dots,n>$ all posible permutation. $n!$. We say that random is that each permutation has the  same probability to occur.
