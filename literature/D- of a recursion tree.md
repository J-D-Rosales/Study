A recursion tree is a tree that represents the recursion of some function or statement. His parts are:
- The root: Represents the cost at the top level of recursion.
- The son's (which are nodes) represents the cost of a single subproblem somewhere in the set of recursive functions invocations.
- The leafs represent the base case of the recursions.
