<span style="color:yellow">IDEA:</span>
The special case relies when the head point to the last part of the input. When it's the rightmost part, and the leftmost part.

<span style="color:yellow">Evidencia:</span>
For example:
$$
q_{i}buv
$$
and we make $\delta(q_{i},b) =(q_{j},c,L)$. then the head that was pointing to b, now points to black, and the b now is c. 
For the rightmost part would be
$$
uvbq_{i}
$$
if we do the same but for the right, we make $uvbcq_{i}$.
