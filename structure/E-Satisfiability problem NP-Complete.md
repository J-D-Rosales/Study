The problem is the next:
Imagine a Boolean algebra or equation, such as:
$$
\phi  = (\bar{x} \land  y) \lor (x \land \bar{z})
$$
Then we say this is satisfiable if there are some assignments to variables with 0 or 1 that led the equation to result in 1. 
Finally the satisfiability problem is to test whether a Boolean expression is satisfiable:
$$
SAT= \{ \langle \phi\rangle | \phi \text{ is a satisfiable Boolean formula} \}
$$
From here we get the formula to connect all NP problems to to the complexity of SAT
