# Complexity theory
Some problems can be hard or easy. For example ordering is easy, but scheduling 1000 curses are hard. 
¿What makes  some problems computationally  hard and others are easy?
It is difficult to say. But in general terms, depend on the way we get to the solution. If it's difficult to get to the answer, in a way of computational time, we could say is hard. 
## Computability theory
It was discovered  there are certain porblems, like prove that a mathematical prove is correct that cannot be done by a computer. 
The theories of computability and complexity are closely related. In com plexity theory, the objective is to classify problems as easy ones and hard ones; whereas in computability theory, the classification of problems is by those that are solvable and those that are not.
